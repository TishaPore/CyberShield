02 / DOCUMENT SAFETY

Keep important files safer

This first version provides a practical safety checklist. It does not upload, store, or encrypt files.

Safety checklist
▤

Make a backup

Keep another copy of essential documents on a separate, trusted drive or reputable cloud account.

⌑

Lock your account

Use a strong unique password and turn on multi-factor authentication where available.

⟳

Test recovery

Occasionally check that you can open your backup and recover a file when needed.

🔒
Privacy by design

Never upload identity documents to an unknown website. This prototype does not ask you to select or upload files.

03 / QUICK GUIDANCE

Four habits that reduce risk

01

Verify independently

Contact the organization using its official website or the number printed on your card—not the message link.

02

Never share OTPs

Don't share passwords, OTPs, PINs, or recovery codes with callers or message senders.

03

Inspect links

Check the actual domain carefully. Don't open unexpected shortened links or attachments.

04

Report suspected fraud

In India, you can report cybercrime at cybercrime.gov.in. For financial cyber fraud, call 1930 promptly.
